# Data extract — live production database (climate-risk-unified on Heroku)

**Extract date:** 14 August 2026
**Source:** Live production Postgres (last geo-risk calculation 12 Aug 2026; supply-chain 3 Aug 2026; management scores 12 Aug 2026)
**Model version:** V6 Probabilistic (v7 Methodology)
**Universe:** 2,454 companies in database; 1,992-issuer **canonical universe** (excludes Financials, incomplete data, Adj/EV > 100%) — flagged per row, not pre-filtered.

All figures replicate the deployed application's exact calculation logic:
- Direct exposure PV = Σ asset-level 30-yr PV, scaled to stated total asset value (`total_asset_value / Σ modelled asset values`).
- Supply-chain PV includes the saturation cap where supplier costs exceed EV.
- Management score = weighted score where available, else equal-weighted (0–100), matching the app.
- Adjusted PV = Total PV × (1 − 0.7 × mgmt score/100).
- VE % fields are **percentages** (e.g. `8.2` = 8.2%), USD fields are decimal dollars. Missing values are empty cells.

## Files

### issuer_universe.csv (2,454 rows) — request A + Section 3 CSV
One row per issuer. Includes gross & adjusted VE %, direct/supply-chain USD split (gross and mgmt-adjusted),
management scores, top hazard + share, per-hazard EAL breakdown, geocoding & valuation confidence (0–100),
model version, and universe flags (`is_financial`, `incomplete_data`, `adj_ve_over_100pct`, `in_canonical_universe`).

**Not available in the model** (Section 3 spec columns we cannot supply): `ticker`, `market_cap_usd`
(only enterprise value is stored), GICS L1/L2 (sector/industry follow the app's own taxonomy),
a formal `scenario` label, or a single fixed `valuation_date` (per-row calculation timestamps exist instead).

### sector_aggregates.csv (10 sectors) — request B
Canonical universe only. EV-weighted and median adjusted VE %, direct vs supply-chain share, top two hazards.

### country_aggregates.csv (47 countries) — request C
By issuer HQ country (canonical universe). Absolute adjusted exposure PV, EV-weighted VE %, top hazard + share.
Note: this is HQ-domicile attribution, not asset-location attribution.

### management_categories.csv — request E
Category-level pass rates (score > 0) across the universe, plus top and bottom adjusted-VE-quintile pass rates.
Two extra category names ("Operational Resilience & Adaptation", "Supply Chain & Stakeholder Engagement")
come from a newer scorecard vintage covering only 4 companies — check `n_scored_items_universe` before using.

### pv_sensitivity_grid.csv (36 rows) — request F
PV parameter sensitivity grid recomputed offline from stored expected annual losses (no hazard-model rerun),
generated by `scripts/pv_sensitivity_grid.mjs`. Grid: discount rate r ∈ {6,8,10,12}%, growth g ∈ {2,4,6}%,
horizon T ∈ {20,30,50} years, PV = EAL × Σ_{t=1..T} (1+g)^(t-1)/(1+r)^t — the exact formula the model uses
(stored defaults verified: direct = A(5%, 0%, 30y) = 15.372; supply chain = A(10%, 4%, 30y) = 13.57).
Columns: `universe_ve_pct` (EV-weighted adjusted VE over the fixed 1,992-issuer canonical universe),
`median_issuer_ve_pct`, `rank_correlation_vs_default` (Spearman ρ of issuer adjusted-VE ranks vs the stored
default parameterisation). **Result: ρ = 0.9995 in every grid cell** — level shifts (universe VE ranges
4.2%–23.2% across the grid) but rank ordering is effectively invariant to r/g/T, supporting the report's claim.
(ρ is not exactly 1 only because the stored defaults discount direct and supply-chain losses at different
rates, while each grid cell applies a single parameterisation to both.)
